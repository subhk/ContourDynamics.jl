Vortex Merger

Two identical nested vortices are placed three radii apart, following the symmetric vortex merger setup in Fig. 13 of Dritschel (1988). Each vortex uses two contour levels to approximate $\omega(r) = 2\pi(1-r^2)$.

What to look for:

  • nested contours stretch and exchange filaments
  • surgery reconnects nearby segments
  • the final state has fewer contours than the initial state

The topological reconnection used for this partial-merger calculation is described in Dritschel (1988) and Dritschel (1989). The full script in examples/vortex_merger.jl writes snapshots, a final figure, an MP4 animation, and vortex_merger_diagnostics.csv with contour count, node count, area, circulation drift, and energy drift for validation.
